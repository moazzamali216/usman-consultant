About Usman Consultants

Our Story

Usman Consultants was established in Faisalabad in 2012 with a clear mission: to deliver premium construction chemicals, structural repair solutions, and surface treatments that extend the life of buildings and infrastructure. What began as a specialized consultancy has grown into a trusted supplier and technical partner for contractors, industrial owners, and government projects across Pakistan.

Under the leadership of Director Usman Zahid, we have built a reputation for reliability, quality assurance, and technical excellence. Our expertise spans structural repair and anchoring, waterproofing systems, industrial flooring, joint sealants, and road marking paints — all engineered to meet the rigorous demands of modern construction.

Today, Usman Consultants operates as a single-source technical partner, offering a comprehensive catalog of high-performance materials. From admixtures and waterproofing to flooring and protective coatings, we deliver consistent quality, technical excellence, and reliable delivery — managing projects of all scales while maintaining the highest standards of safety and durability.
