External Protection

Membrane Sheets

Physical Barrier Systems

Premium PVC, HDPE, and geo-membranes providing physical waterproofing barriers. Used in tunnels, basements, ponds, landfills, and civil engineering projects.

PVC HDPE Geo-Membrane

Drainage Boards

Water Management

High-performance drainage boards for foundation walls, retaining walls, and green roofs. Effectively channel water away from structures to prevent hydrostatic pressure.

Membrane Protection

Protective boards designed to shield waterproofing membranes during backfilling and construction. Prevents mechanical damage and extends membrane service life.

Impact Resistant Durable Easy Install

Swell Bars

Self-Sealing Technology

Hydrophilic swellable water bars that expand upon contact with water to create a watertight seal. Ideal for construction joints, pipe penetrations, and cold joints.

Water-Based Economical Easy Apply

Hot Bitumen

Frequently Asked Questions

We offer a comprehensive range of waterproofing systems including positive and negative waterproofing, cementitious coatings, epoxy coatings, PU coatings, membrane sheets (PVC, HDPE, geo-membranes), drainage boards, protection boards, water stoppers, swell bars, bitumen emulsion, hot bitumen, fiber coatings, rubber coatings, and zinc enrich primer.

For roofs, we recommend PU coatings, fiber coatings, or hot bitumen systems depending on the roof type, slope, and exposure conditions. PU coatings offer excellent flexibility and UV resistance, while fiber coatings provide enhanced crack-bridging capabilities. Our technical team can recommend the optimal solution for your specific roof requirements.

Positive waterproofing is applied on the exterior (water-facing) surface to prevent water ingress, ideal for new construction and areas with external access. Negative waterproofing is applied on the interior surface to seal out water that has already penetrated, ideal for existing structures and areas where exterior access is limited. Both systems are available from Usman Consultants.

Yes, we offer low-VOC and eco-friendly formulations across our product range. Many of our waterproofing systems are water-based and contain reduced volatile organic compounds, making them safer for applicators and the environment. We are committed to sustainable construction practices and continuously work to improve the environmental profile of our products.

Yes, Usman Consultants has skilled application teams trained in the proper installation of all waterproofing systems. We provide on-site supervision, surface preparation, application guidance, and post-installation verification to ensure optimal performance and durability of all waterproofing solutions.
