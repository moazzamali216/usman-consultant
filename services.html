WHAT WE OFFER

Our Premium Services

Complete construction chemical solutions — from admixtures and waterproofing to industrial flooring and protective coatings. Engineered for durability, strength, and long-lasting performance.

01

Concrete Admixtures

High-performance plasticizers, superplasticizers, retarders, accelerators, and waterproofing additives engineered to enhance workability, durability, and strength.

02

Waterproofing Systems

Comprehensive waterproofing for roofs, overhead & underground tanks, wastewater plants, and structures. Cementitious coatings, membrane sheets, and low-VOC formulations.

03

Industrial Flooring

Epoxy, PU, self-leveling, and heavy-duty flooring solutions. Engineered to withstand heavy machinery, chemical exposure, and high-traffic conditions.

04

Joint Sealants

Premium PU, Bitumastic, Acrylic, and Polysulphide sealants for expansion, contraction, and construction joints — suitable for runways, deck ways, and concrete structures.

05

Protective Coatings & Paints

High-performance paints, road marking, and protective coatings with UV resistance, anti-slip properties, and fast-drying formulations. Available in low-VOC eco-friendly options.

06

Structural Repair & Anchoring

Concrete repairs, grouts, anchors, and reinforcement solutions to restore structural integrity, improve stability, and extend building lifespan.

07

Surface Treatments

Advanced surface preparation, treatment, and finishing solutions for concrete, metal, and masonry surfaces. Ensuring optimal adhesion, durability, and aesthetic appeal.

08

Adhesives & Bonding Agents

High-strength structural adhesives, bonding agents, and chemical anchors for concrete, steel, and masonry applications. Ensuring superior bonding strength and durability.

09

Membrane Systems

PVC membranes, HDPE membranes, geo-membranes, and drainage boards for waterproofing, lining, and protection applications. Suitable for tunnels, basements, and civil engineering projects.

10

Asphalts & Bitumen Products

Premium asphalt, bitumen emulsion, hot bitumen, and fiber coatings for waterproofing, road construction, and protective applications. High-quality formulations for durability.

11

Grouts & Anchors

High-strength cementitious and epoxy grouts, chemical anchors, and injection systems for structural reinforcement, crack repair, and equipment installation.

12

Concrete Repairs

Complete concrete repair solutions including crack injection, spall repair, corrosion control, and surface restoration. Restoring structural integrity and extending service life.
