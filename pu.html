Polyether-type PU

Flexible · Hydrolytic Stability

Polyether polyurethanes are known for their excellent hydrolytic stability, flexibility at low temperatures, and resistance to microbial attack. They offer superior performance in applications requiring long-term durability in wet or humid environments.

Key Advantages

Hydrolytic Stability Low Temp Flexibility Microbial Resistance Flexible

Typical Applications

Flexible Foams Sealants Adhesives Coatings

Polyester-type PU

Strength · Chemical Resistance

Polyester polyurethanes offer superior mechanical strength, excellent chemical resistance, and high abrasion resistance. They perform exceptionally well in demanding environments where durability and structural integrity are critical.

Key Advantages

High Mechanical Strength Chemical Resistance Abrasion Resistance Structural Integrity

Typical Applications

Rigid Foams Elastomers Industrial Coatings Automotive Parts

PU Dispersions (PUD)

Water-based · Eco-friendly

Polyurethane dispersions are water-based systems that combine the performance of PU with environmental benefits. Available in anionic, cationic, or non-ionic variants, they offer low VOC emissions and excellent film-forming properties.

Key Advantages

Low VOC Eco-friendly Excellent Film Formation Versatile

Complete PU Portfolio

Polyurethane —
One Material. Infinite Solutions.

From thermal insulation to waterproofing, from protective coatings to structural repair — polyurethane's versatility makes it the material of choice across construction and industry. Explore our complete range below.

Polyurethane (PU) is one of the most versatile material families in the world. Its unique chemistry allows it to be formulated into a vast range of products — from flexible foams to rigid insulation, from tough coatings to elastic adhesives. At Usman Consultants, we offer a comprehensive portfolio of PU solutions engineered for construction, infrastructure, and industrial applications.

Thermal Insulation Systems — Our PU insulation solutions include spray foam (open-cell and closed-cell), rigid PIR/PUR boards, and structural insulated panels (SIPs). With thermal conductivity as low as 0.022 W/m·K, these systems deliver exceptional energy efficiency for walls, roofs, floors, and façades. Closed-cell spray foam also provides vapor barrier protection, while open-cell foam offers superior sound absorption.

Waterproofing & Sealing Systems — Our liquid-applied PU membranes provide seamless, flexible, and durable waterproofing for roofs, balconies, basements, and wet areas. With elongation exceeding 500% and cold-applied application, these systems adapt to structural movement and complex geometries. PU sealants for expansion joints, movement joints, and crack sealing ensure long-term watertight integrity.

Protective Coatings & Linings — Our high-performance PU coatings deliver exceptional corrosion resistance, chemical resistance, and UV stability for steel structures, pipelines, marine environments, and industrial equipment. With service life exceeding 20 years and resistance to extreme temperatures (-30°C to 600°C), these coatings protect assets in the most demanding environments.

Industrial Flooring Systems — Our seamless PU flooring systems provide exceptional durability, chemical resistance, and hygiene for factories, warehouses, clean rooms, food processing plants, and pharmaceutical facilities. With high compressive strength and abrasion resistance, these floors withstand heavy loads and continuous traffic.

Structural Strengthening & Repair — Our PU-based repair systems include low-viscosity crack injection resins, high-strength structural adhesives for rebar anchoring, and composite systems for seismic retrofitting and load capacity enhancement. These solutions restore and extend the life of concrete and masonry structures.

Specialty PU Products — Our portfolio also includes PU elastomers for vibration damping, fire-retardant PU systems for passive fire protection, heat-resistant coatings for industrial ovens and chimneys, and anti-corrosion systems for marine and offshore applications.

Every PU product is engineered to meet international standards including EN 1504, ASTM, and ISO 9001 — with SGS testing for quality assurance and performance verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

We offer a comprehensive range of polyurethane (PU) products including spray foam insulation, rigid insulation boards, liquid waterproofing membranes, joint sealants, protective coatings, industrial floor coatings, structural adhesives, and elastomeric systems — each engineered for specific construction and industrial applications.

Open-cell spray foam is lightweight, flexible, and provides excellent sound absorption — ideal for interior walls and attics. Closed-cell spray foam is denser, provides superior thermal insulation (R-value), acts as a vapor barrier, and adds structural strength — ideal for exterior walls, roofs, and below-grade applications. Each serves different performance requirements.

PU waterproofing systems offer seamless application, exceptional flexibility (>500% elongation), excellent adhesion to various substrates, and long-term durability (25+ years). They are cold-applied, require no heat, and can be applied to complex geometries. Unlike traditional sheet membranes, PU systems provide monolithic protection with no seams or joints — eliminating potential failure points.

PU protective coatings offer exceptional corrosion resistance, chemical resistance, and UV stability. They provide excellent adhesion to steel, concrete, and various substrates, with high abrasion resistance and flexibility. With service life exceeding 20 years and resistance to extreme temperatures (-30°C to 600°C), PU coatings are ideal for pipelines, marine structures, industrial equipment, and infrastructure projects.

Our technical team assesses your project requirements including substrate type, environmental conditions, performance needs (thermal insulation, waterproofing, corrosion protection), and application method. We provide detailed proposals with product specifications, application guidelines, and compliance documentation. Contact us for a free consultation.

All our PU products meet international standards including EN 1504 (concrete protection), ASTM C881 (epoxy resins), ISO 9001 certified, and are SGS tested for quality assurance. Products comply with low VOC environmental standards and are tested for thermal performance, water resistance, UV stability, and mechanical properties.
