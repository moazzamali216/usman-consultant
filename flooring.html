Frequently Asked Questions

We offer a comprehensive range of industrial and commercial flooring systems including epoxy flooring, polyurethane (PU) flooring, self-leveling floors, heavy-duty flooring, and floor repair & joint treatment solutions — each engineered for specific performance requirements and environmental conditions.

Epoxy flooring offers exceptional chemical resistance, high compressive strength (80+ MPa), and is ideal for heavy industrial environments. PU flooring provides superior flexibility (>500% elongation), excellent impact resistance, and UV stability — making it perfect for outdoor parking structures, warehouses, and areas with thermal movement.

Self-leveling flooring is a high-flow compound that creates perfectly flat, smooth, and seamless surfaces. It is ideal for commercial spaces, showrooms, healthcare facilities, and as a base for other floor coverings. It can be applied up to 10mm thickness and is walkable within 4-6 hours.

For heavy industrial use, we recommend our heavy-duty flooring systems or high-build epoxy flooring. These systems offer compressive strength exceeding 80 MPa, withstand heavy machinery, forklift traffic, impact loads, and chemical exposure. They provide 30+ years of service life in demanding environments.

Usman Consultants offers complete floor repair solutions including epoxy injection for cracks, high-strength repair mortars for spalls and surface defects, self-leveling compounds for uneven surfaces, and protective coatings for extended durability. Our repair systems cure in 24-48 hours, minimizing downtime. Contact us for a professional assessment.

Our technical team assesses your project requirements including load conditions, chemical exposure, temperature variations, traffic levels, and aesthetic preferences to recommend the optimal flooring system. We provide detailed proposals with application guidelines, curing times, and maintenance recommendations. Contact us for a free consultation.
