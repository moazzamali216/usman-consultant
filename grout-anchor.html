KNOWLEDGE CENTER

Frequently Asked Questions

We offer a comprehensive range of grouting and anchoring systems including cementitious grouts, epoxy grouts, PU grouts, chemical anchors (polyester, epoxy, hybrid), and mechanical anchors (wedge, sleeve, screw) — each engineered for specific performance requirements and load conditions.

Cementitious grouts are non-shrink, high-strength (80+ MPa) grouts ideal for precision equipment anchoring. Epoxy grouts offer exceptional chemical resistance and compressive strength exceeding 100 MPa for heavy-duty applications. PU grouts provide superior flexibility (>500% elongation), impact resistance, and vibration damping for dynamic applications.

Chemical anchors use resin (polyester, epoxy, or hybrid) to bond the anchor to the substrate, offering high load capacity (10-200+ kN) and are ideal for structural connections and rebar fixing. Mechanical anchors (wedge, sleeve, screw) provide instant fixing without curing time, suitable for general construction and medium-duty applications.

For heavy equipment anchoring, we recommend precision cementitious grouts (80+ MPa) or epoxy grouts (100+ MPa). These non-shrink formulations provide exceptional load transfer, dimensional stability, and chemical resistance — ensuring reliable performance for heavy machinery, compressors, and industrial equipment.

Our technical team assesses your project requirements including load capacity, substrate type, environmental conditions, and installation method to recommend the optimal anchoring system. We provide detailed proposals with specifications, installation guidelines, and compliance documentation. Contact us for a free consultation.

All our grouting and anchoring products meet international standards including EN 1504, ASTM C1107 (cementitious grouts), ASTM C881 (epoxy grouts), EN 1992-4 (anchors), and are ISO 9001 certified. Products are SGS tested for quality assurance and performance verification.
